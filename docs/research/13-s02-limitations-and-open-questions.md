# S02 limitations and open questions

S02 establishes a reference measurement kernel for a bounded subset. It does not establish that the seven S01 dimensions are latent variables, that any observation is pedagogically useful or that a policy improves learning.

Open questions include cross-language specifications, parser-independent oracles, formatter and template sensitivity, clone leakage, task-level invariance, measurement redundancy, reviewer comprehension, accessibility, fairness, burden, process-evidence integration and the validity of action policies.

The public v2.2.0 route remains unchanged. S04 will integrate a validated subset into a new report route only after S03 robustness results justify the transition. `A01-F003` remains open because this phase does not prove universal process containment.
