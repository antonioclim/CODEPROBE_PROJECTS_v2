# Prospective robustness hypotheses

The hypotheses in `research/s02-robustness-hypotheses.v1.json` are prospective plans. They are neither results nor a preregistration record.

The principal transformation families are path renaming, newline conversion, BOM insertion, alpha renaming, comment insertion, formatter application, syntax corruption, unsupported-language declaration, template injection, duplicate contamination and task/language distribution shift.

Each hypothesis has a falsification rule. Violations will be reported rather than reclassified after observing the data. S03 owns computational metamorphic execution; E01 owns corpus-level nuisance and generalisation analyses.
