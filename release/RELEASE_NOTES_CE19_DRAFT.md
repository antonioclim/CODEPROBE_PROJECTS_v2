# CodeProbe CE19 source-only research candidate

This draft release packages the bounded evidence-contract implementation reported in the Software Quality Journal manuscript. It separates measurement, interpretation policy, source evidence and consumer admission and preserves explicit provenance boundaries.

## Candidate release contents

- source-only CodeProbe implementation under the existing MIT licence;
- schemas and research contracts;
- repository metadata, citation metadata and provenance records.

## Explicit exclusions

The candidate does not contain the legacy global-scalar application, article manuscript, editable figures, Ruff/Radon binaries, the private CE11 executable replay, private continuity archives or a DOI. The separately assembled reproducibility and figure assets remain held until their public licences and distribution boundaries are explicitly approved.

CodeProbe is not an AI-authorship or misconduct detector.
