# Reproducibility boundary

This repository contains the complete maintained software snapshot and a bounded evidence-contract source route. It is not the complete executable evidence archive used for every manuscript result.

- **CE09** is the bounded table-reconstruction and native-slice qualification package. Its exact archive remains separately identified and is not attached to the software release.
- **CE11** is the historical replay package. It retains the S05 source, comparator material and the original negative result. It remains private while third-party notice, source-availability and data-licence holds are open.
- **CE04** is the locally repaired candidate used by the bounded scientific route. It is a separate source identity from historical S05 and must not be substituted into historical records.
- **The v2.3.0 bounded source-only asset** exposes only the admitted source, schemas, research contracts and public provenance limits. Its manifest and SHA-256 are separate from the complete tagged repository snapshot.

The principal denominators remain separate: 1,101 original predicates (1,100 true and one false), 1,357 defined record comparisons, 288 decision comparisons and 24 blocked pseudo-source reads. They must not be combined, and the failed invalid-UTF-8/C901-only predicate must not be converted into a pass.

A successful local run establishes only the behaviour exercised by its exact source, platform, runtime and command. It does not establish universal containment, independent-team reproduction, empirical educational utility or external validity. The complete claim–artefact–command–result matrix and held executable packages remain outside this repository release.
