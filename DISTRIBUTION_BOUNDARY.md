# Distribution boundary

## Included in the source-only candidate

- the exact 28-component CE04 native slice;
- one unchanged support-only view oracle;
- schemas, research contracts, source-level documentation and repository metadata created for CE19.

## Excluded

- the complete 348-file legacy application and its global-scalar interface;
- the article manuscript, editable figures and editorial files;
- all Ruff wheels and Rust crate archives;
- Radon or Ruff executable/source distributions;
- the exact CE11 executable replay package;
- private handover archives, synthetic owner returns and internal editorial audits;
- any DOI, release tag or statement that a remote repository already contains this candidate.

The exclusion of held third-party executables makes CE13-N01/N02 not applicable to this source-only candidate. It does not close those holds for the separately preserved CE11 executable archive. The manuscript and figures are distributed only through separately identified packages and retain their own publication/licensing boundary.
